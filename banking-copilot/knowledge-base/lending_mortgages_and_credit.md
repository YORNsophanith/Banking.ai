# KEY Bank — Lending, Mortgages & Credit Underwriting Knowledge Base

## 1. Credit Facilities & Lending Portfolio

### A. Home Mortgage Loans (កម្ចីទិញគេហដ្ឋាន)
- **Base Interest Rate:** Starting at **6.99% p.a.** (Fixed for the initial 24 months, thereafter variable at KEY Prime Rate + 2.50%).
- **Green Eco-Home Mortgage Promotion:** **6.50% p.a.** for properties equipped with certified solar rooftop systems or energy-efficient certifications.
- **Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV):**
  - **Hard Title (L-Map Certificate):** Up to **80%** of independent certified appraisal value.
  - **Soft Title (Sangkat/Khan Registration):** Up to **65%** of appraised value.
- **Maximum Tenure:** Up to **25 Years (300 Months)** for residential home purchases; up to 15 years for land acquisition.
- **Repayment Methods:**
  - Standard Annuity (Equal monthly installments of Principal + Interest).
  - Equal Principal Repayment with declining interest.
- **Prepayment & Early Settlement Policy:**
  - Years 1–3: 2.0% penalty fee on the prepaid principal amount.
  - After Year 3: **0% (Free of charge)** for partial or full early settlement.

### B. Personal Unsecured Consumer Loans (កម្ចីផ្ទាល់ខ្លួនគ្មានទ្រព្យធានា)
- **Borrowing Limit:** **$1,000 to $25,000 USD** (or up to 100,000,000 KHR).
- **Collateral:** **100% Zero Collateral Required**.
- **Tenure:** 12 to 60 Months.
- **Interest Rate:** 9.99% to 12.50% p.a. based on CBC credit rating and employer tier.
- **Eligibility Criteria:**
  - Minimum verified monthly net salary: **$400.00 USD**.
  - Minimum employment tenure: 6 consecutive months with current employer.
  - Clean Credit Bureau Cambodia (CBC) record with 0 days past due (DPD 30+) in past 12 months.

### C. SME & Commercial Business Financing (កម្ចីអាជីវកម្ម និងសហគ្រាស)
- **Facility Size:** **$10,000 to $1,000,000 USD+** (Tailored credit facilities).
- **Products:**
  - Revolving Overdraft (OD) Facility for working capital (interest billed on daily drawn balance).
  - Term Loans for machinery, commercial vehicle acquisition, or factory expansion (up to 10 years).
  - Letters of Credit (LC) and Bank Guarantees for international trade.
- **Interest Rates:** From **7.50% p.a.** (USD) / **8.50% p.a.** (KHR).

## 2. Underwriting Criteria & Risk Management

### A. Debt-Service Ratio (DSR) Underwriting Ceiling
- **Strict DSR Cap:** **50% of verified monthly net income**.
- **Calculation Formula:**
  $$\text{DSR} = \frac{\text{Total Monthly Debt Obligations (Existing CBC Debts + Requested KEY Bank Loan)}}{\text{Verified Net Monthly Income}} \times 100\%$$
- **Example:** A borrower with $2,000/month net income cannot have total monthly debt payments exceeding $1,000/month.

### B. Credit Bureau Cambodia (CBC) Scoring Bands
- **Grade A (CBC Score 720–850):** Super-Prime. Instant pre-approval, lowest risk margins.
- **Grade B (CBC Score 640–719):** Prime. Standard approval with standard documentation.
- **Grade C (CBC Score 580–639):** Near-Prime. Requires additional collateral margin or guarantor.
- **Grade D/E (CBC Score < 580 or Active DPD 30+):** Elevated risk; subject to mandatory senior credit committee review.

### C. Required Application Documentation Checklist
1. **Identification:** Valid Cambodian National ID Card or International Passport with valid visa.
2. **Residency Proof:** Family Book (សៀវភៅគ្រួសារ), Residence Book (សៀវភៅស្នាក់នៅ), or Sangkat Certificate.
3. **Income Verification:**
   - Employed: Last 6 months bank statement showing salary credits + Employment letter / Salary slips.
   - Self-Employed / Business Owner: Patent tax certificate, business registration, last 6 months business bank statements, profit/loss ledger.
4. **Property Valuation:** Copy of property title deed (Hard Title / Soft Title) for valuation dispatch.
