# KEY Bank — Retail & Commercial Banking Products Knowledge Base (Enterprise Edition)

## 1. Deposit Accounts & Yield Schedules

### A. Everyday Checking Account (គណនីចរន្តទូទៅ)
- **Monthly Maintenance Fee:** $0.00 (No minimum monthly balance required).
- **Opening Balance Requirement:** $10.00 USD / 40,000 KHR.
- **Features & Peripherals:** Linked Visa Platinum Debit Card, instant Bakong KHQR transfers, zero fee for mobile banking, monthly consolidated e-Statements, real-time SMS & Push notifications.
- **Overdraft Protection:** Optional linked line of credit up to $1,000 USD to prevent bounced payments ($0 overdraft transfer fee).

### B. High-Yield Savings Account (គណនីសន្សំការប្រាក់ខ្ពស់)
- **Annual Percentage Yield (APY):** **4.75% p.a. (USD)** | **5.25% p.a. (KHR)**
- **Interest Calculation Model:** Calculated daily on closing ledger balance; compounded and credited on the last calendar day of each month.
- **Minimum Opening Balance:** $50.00 USD / 200,000 KHR.
- **Withdrawal Terms:** Unlimited free withdrawals and transfers through Mobile Banking and ATM networks.

## 2. Domestic & Cross-Border Payment Infrastructure

### A. Bakong KHQR & Domestic Interbank Settlement
- **KEY Bank Internal Transfers:** **$0.00 (100% Free)**, instant 24/7/365.
- **Bakong KHQR Standard Transfer / Merchant Pay:** **$0.00 (100% Free)** to all commercial banks and MDIs across Cambodia.
- **Daily Retail Limit:** $10,000 USD / 40,000,000 KHR per day (Tier 1 KYC). Extended to $50,000 USD / 200,000,000 KHR (Tier 2 Full KYC).

### B. Bakong Cross-Border QR Remittance (Regional Interoperability)
- **Supported Corridors:**
  1. **Cambodia ↔ Thailand (PromptPay):** Direct real-time KHQR scanning in Thailand at over 8 million merchants.
  2. **Cambodia ↔ Vietnam (NAPAS):** Cross-border payment support at participating Vietnamese merchants.
  3. **Cambodia ↔ Laos (LAPNet):** Instant QR settlement in LAK and KHR.
  4. **Cambodia ↔ Malaysia (DuitNow):** Seamless QR payment integration.
- **FX Conversion:** Favorable mid-market wholesale exchange rates with zero transaction surcharge.

### C. International SWIFT Wire Remittance
- **Outbound SWIFT Wire Transfer:** **$25.00 USD flat fee** + correspondent bank fee ($15–$30 depending on routing).
- **Inbound SWIFT Wire Transfer:** **$5.00 USD inward handling fee**.
- **Processing SLA:** Same-day processing for wires submitted before 15:30 (GMT+7). Standard execution timeline 1–3 business days.
- **Mandatory Information for Outbound Wire:**
  - Beneficiary Full Name and Registered Residential/Corporate Address.
  - Beneficiary Account Number / IBAN.
  - Receiving Bank SWIFT BIC Code (8 or 11 characters).
  - Clear Economic Purpose Code (e.g., Education, Trade, Property Purchase, Family Support).
