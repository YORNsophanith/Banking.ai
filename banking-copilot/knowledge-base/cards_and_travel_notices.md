# Bank Customer Policy: Payment Cards, PINs & International Travel

## 1. International Travel Notices
- Customers traveling abroad can set up to 5 destination countries and travel dates (up to 90 days in advance).
- Setting a travel notice prevents legitimate overseas transactions from being flagged or declined by automated fraud neural engines.
- Emergency overseas phone numbers and contactless wallet provisioning (Apple Pay / Google Wallet) remain active globally.

## 2. Card Status Options & Controls
- **FROZEN (Temporary Lock)**:
  - Can be toggled on/off instantly.
  - Blocks new retail purchases, online checkouts, and ATM withdrawals.
  - Pre-scheduled recurring utility/mortgage bills will still clear.
- **PERMANENTLY_BLOCKED (Stolen / Lost)**:
  - Irreversible. Destroys the digital card number token.
  - Reorders a new physical EMV chip card with a new card number to the primary address on file.
- **PIN Reset**:
  - Customers can reset their 4-digit debit PIN after completing SMS Step-Up verification.

## 3. Contactless & Digital Wallet
- Instant issuance to Apple Wallet / Google Pay is available immediately after requesting a replacement card, even before physical delivery.
