# Bank Customer Policy: Account Limits, Transfers & Fee Schedule

## 1. Account Types & Daily Transaction Limits
- **Everyday Checking**:
  - Daily ATM Cash Withdrawal Limit: $1,000 USD
  - Daily Point-of-Sale (POS) / Debit Purchase Limit: $3,000 USD
  - Outbound Online ACH Transfer Limit: $5,000 USD per business day / $25,000 monthly
- **Premier Platinum Checking**:
  - Daily ATM Cash Withdrawal Limit: $2,500 USD
  - Daily POS / Debit Purchase Limit: $10,000 USD
  - Outbound Online ACH Transfer Limit: $25,000 USD per business day
- **High-Yield Savings**:
  - Federal Reserve limit of up to 6 convenient monthly withdrawals.

## 2. Standard Service Fees
- **Monthly Maintenance Fee**:
  - Everyday Checking: $12.00 (Waived with minimum daily balance of $1,500 or monthly qualifying direct deposits of $500+).
  - High-Yield Savings: $0.00 (No monthly fee).
- **Overdraft / Insufficient Funds (NSF)**:
  - $34.00 per item (Maximum 3 fees per business day). Overdraft Grace Period applies if balance is brought positive by 8:00 PM EST same day.
- **Domestic Outgoing Wire Transfer**: $25.00
- **International Outgoing Wire Transfer**: $45.00 + foreign exchange margin.
- **Out-of-Network ATM Surcharge**: $3.00 (Premier accounts receive full rebate at statement cycle end).

## 3. Temporary Limit Increases
Customers can request a temporary 24-hour limit increase (up to $5,000 for ATM or $15,000 for POS) directly through the AI Banking Assistant provided the account has been in good standing for at least 90 days and passes two-factor biometric/SMS OTP verification.
